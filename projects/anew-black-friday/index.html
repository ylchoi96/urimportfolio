ANEW GOLF / Campaign experience / 2026

FOUR DAYS.
ONE BLACK FRIDAY.

A bold campaign identity carried from the storefront to daily benefits, timed product releases, and mobile shopping.

PROJECT

2026 Black Friday
October 29 – November 1

ROLE

Campaign design
Responsive publishing & operations

PLATFORM

Cafe24 official store
Desktop & mobile

DELIVERABLES

Campaign landing / Time sale
Store banners / Last Piece

01 / Campaign system

ONE IDENTITY.
MULTIPLE ENTRY POINTS.

Distressed red textures, oversized black typography, and star motifs establish the main campaign. The time sale extends the same language through dark textures and red accents, while Last Piece gives limited-stock products a distinct destination.

02 / Benefits landing

MAKE THE NEXT
ACTION CLEAR.

The landing page brings the campaign deadline, daily coupon, time sale, and Last Piece into one hierarchy. On mobile, the benefit cards stack vertically so each offer and its action stay together.

03 / Time-sale experience

TIME CHANGES.
THE INTERFACE FOLLOWS.

A countdown and four-day schedule communicate the next release. The captured states show how the page moves from an opening notice to a live product lineup, with sold-out products visibly differentiated.

01 / STATUS

Upcoming & active releases

Schedule cards distinguish upcoming slots from the current sale with a red highlight and explicit status text.

02 / PRODUCT INFORMATION

Readable prices

Campaign display fixes addressed duplicate price and discount information, with the mobile layout checked alongside desktop.

03 / AVAILABILITY

Visible sold-out feedback

A dimmed product image and red SOLD OUT overlay communicate availability directly within the product grid.

Screenshots captured October 29, 2026. Tabs switch between archived screens; timers and product availability are preserved as captured.

04 / Last Piece

ANOTHER WAY
INTO THE CAMPAIGN.

A dedicated Last Piece banner connects the campaign to the product catalogue. The desktop horizontal banner becomes a compact portrait treatment on mobile.

Design into operation

BUILT FOR
A LIVE STORE.

The work extended beyond campaign visuals into responsive page implementation, scheduled display checks, product presentation fixes, and operational handover.

  • Connected storefront banners and campaign destinations.
  • Adapted landing and product layouts for desktop and mobile.
  • Refined countdown copy, type scale, and sold-out presentation.
  • Documented opening, transition, and post-event restoration checks.

This case study documents delivered screens and operational work. Sales or conversion uplift is not claimed.
