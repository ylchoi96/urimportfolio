02 · Problems & Changes

Three changes that shaped the renewal.

A comparison of the legacy and renewed structures, with the design rationale and tradeoffs made explicit.

01 · Navigation

Replace broad labels with specific product groups.

Before
Categories included Bags, Pouches, Kids, and ETC, alongside shopping and support links.
After
Cooling Bags, Travel, Bags, Fashion, and Living organize the range. Collaboration has its own entry; support content is grouped under ABOUT.
Rationale
Specific labels explain the range more clearly than ETC. A broader menu still needs well-organized subcategories.
02 · Product discovery

Make BEST products visible without a tab choice.

Before
BEST used five hashtag tabs. The retrospective comparison recorded six sold-out items in the picnic-mat tab.
After
BEST SELLERS uses a single product list.
Rationale
Customers can scan products immediately. Category filtering moves out of this module, while featured stock still requires regular maintenance.
03 · Campaign structure

Give curation and promotions different jobs.

Before
TODAY’S P!CK repeated one product across three slots; an empty keyword slot was also recorded.
After
NEW ITEM, SNILLO’s PICK, BEST SELLERS, and PROMOTION form separate sections with defined content roles.
Rationale
Campaign stories, launches, and offers no longer share an unclear slot purpose. Their usefulness depends on keeping product selections and links current.
