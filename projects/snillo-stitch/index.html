02 · Problems & Structure

Three changes that shaped the renewal.

The legacy and renewed structures show what changed. The rationale explains the resulting design tradeoffs.

01 · Navigation

Use specific labels for a broader range.

Before
Six category links included NEW, BEST, Bags, Pouches, Kids, and ETC.
After
Ten top-level entries cover shopping shortcuts, five product groups, collaboration, EVENT, and ABOUT.
Rationale & tradeoff
The menu grows from six category links to ten top-level entries in exchange for more specific labels and a dedicated collaboration path.
02 · Product discovery

Show BEST products without a tab choice.

Before
The legacy BEST module used five hashtag tabs, each with its own product selection.
After
BEST SELLERS presents a single product list.
Rationale & tradeoff
Removing the tab choice makes the shortlist directly visible, but removes category filtering within this module. Category browsing remains available in navigation.
03 · Campaign structure

Separate product lists from campaign roles.

Before
TODAY’S P!CK showed the same product in three slots in the legacy reference.
After
NEW ITEM, PICK, collaboration products, BEST SELLERS, and PROMOTION have distinct content roles.
Rationale & tradeoff
Campaign imagery adds context around products, but also introduces more scrolling between product lists. Each campaign therefore needs a clear collection path.
