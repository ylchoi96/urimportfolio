01 · Context & Role

Renewing an operating store,
within an existing platform.

The work connected brand presentation with the practical requirements of a Cafe24 storefront.

The range covered bags, cooling bags, travel goods, fashion, living products, and collaborations. The renewal reorganized the storefront around these product groups and gave campaigns a defined place alongside product lists.

My contribution covered design, publishing, Cafe24 customization, and QA. This was a theme-based renewal: the legacy footer credits morenvy and the renewed footer credits CLOTHIC. Those theme foundations are acknowledged separately from the project’s design and implementation work.

02 · Problems & Decisions

Three concrete changes.
Three visible tradeoffs.

Observed changes are separated from retrospective design reasoning. The reasoning below explains the resulting structure without presenting inferred intentions as documented project decisions.

Legacy observation Renewed structure Rationale & tradeoff
01 · Navigation

Product-type labels included Bags, Pouches, Kids, and ETC. Shopping and support links sat alongside one another.

Cooling Bags, Travel, Bags, Fashion, and Living organize the range; collaboration has its own entry and support content is grouped under ABOUT.

Reading the result

Specific group labels replace the catch-all ETC entry. The tradeoff is a broader navigation structure that still needs clear subcategories.

02 · Best products

BEST used five hashtag tabs. The comparison document records one picnic-mat tab showing six sold-out items.

BEST SELLERS presents one product list without the old tab selection.

Reading the result

Products can be scanned without selecting a tab first. Category filtering is no longer available inside this module; category navigation remains important. This does not prove stock issues were eliminated.

03 · Campaign curation

TODAY’S P!CK repeated the same product across three slots, and an empty keyword slot was recorded in the comparison.

The homepage separates NEW ITEM, SNILLO’s PICK, BEST SELLERS, and PROMOTION, with a collaboration banner and associated products.

Reading the result

Each area has a distinct content role. This makes the structure easier to explain, but useful curation still depends on keeping its products and links current.

This is a content map of the delivered storefront, not a claim that a new component library or CMS editor was built.

The legacy four-item new-arrival area becomes a horizontal list with a “View all” path. More items can be offered without making every item a separate homepage section.

A single product list replaces the old five-tab BEST arrangement. The module’s role is a shortlist; category browsing belongs in the catalogue.

04 PROMOTION

A feature banner and two event slots separate shopping offers from product lists. The mobile capture below shows the feature campaign followed by the two offers.

Repeatable structure, with an operational dependency The visible pattern is a titled section with a defined product or campaign role. A practical checklist for the next storefront review.

This checklist is a review framework, not a reproduced historical QA log or a claim that every item was formally recorded.

  • Navigation and destinations Check category, collection, promotion, and product links on desktop and mobile.
  • Product states Review unavailable products, empty lists, duplicate selections, and long product names.
  • Campaign updates Replace banner assets and check image proportions, copy length, and target links.
  • Mobile controls Check menus and fixed controls for overlap with content and purchase actions.
10 · Delivered Result & Next Validation

A delivered storefront.
An observable structural change.

The result is a live Cafe24 renewal with reorganized navigation, differentiated homepage sections, and desktop/mobile presentation. Its business impact is a separate question.

01 What changed

The visible category structure now distinguishes five product groups and a collaboration entry. BEST moved from five tabs to one list, and campaigns have dedicated homepage areas.

02 What was delivered

Design and publishing work continued into a production Cafe24 storefront, including catalogue presentation, bag detail content, promotions, and collaboration content.

03 What remains to test

Discovery speed, conversion, campaign engagement, and update-time savings are not established by the captures. No measured improvement is attributed to the renewal.

Project takeaway

This renewal brought product groups, collaboration stories, and promotional offers into a defined storefront structure — and carried that structure through to Cafe24 publishing.
