SK magic

An appliance rental website redesign connecting product discovery, rental options, and applications.

Web & UI designDesktop websiteOriginal Korean interface

Background & goals

A more distinct rental experience

The previous site used a layout shared with another rental brand. The redesign aimed to strengthen SK magic’s identity and improve the visibility of product information, promotions, and rental choices.

01 · Homepage

Discover products and promotions

The homepage brings categories, featured products, promotions, and consultation entry points into a consistent brand presentation.

02 · Product listing

Compare the available products

The listing groups appliances into a product grid so customers can explore the available rental options.

03 · Product detail

Understand the rental options

The product page presents colour choices, rental terms, card discounts, and bundle options alongside consultation and rental actions.

04 · Rental application

A clear application form

The application page brings together the selected product, gift options, customer details, and consent before submission.

05 · Card benefits

Explain the available discounts

The benefits page groups participating cards and their conditions so customers can review the available rental discounts.

Reflection

Documented design work

The original portfolio reports increased enquiries and rental applications after the redesign, but does not include figures or a comparison period. Further evaluation would focus on rental-price clarity and application completion.
